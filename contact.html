Frequently Asked Questions

What is a SpidyWeb "Destination Stack"?

A Destination Stack is an all-inclusive, self-contained tour entity that bundles verified high-resolution photography, vetted 4-star/5-star accommodations, curated sightseeing spots with timings/fees, day-by-day schedules, adventure activities, and authentic cuisine guides with 100% unique details per place.

Are all package prices guaranteed in Indian Rupees (₹)?

Yes, 100%! All package rates, stay options, and activities are quoted in Indian Rupees (INR ₹) inclusive of all applicable GST, permits, toll plazas, and chauffeur allowances with zero hidden fees.

What is the cancellation and refund policy?

We provide 100% full refund for cancellations made up to 7 days before your scheduled departure date. Rescheduling is free of charge up to 72 hours prior to travel.
